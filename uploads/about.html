About me.

Dori is a UX/UI designer based in Prague.

Since 2023, she has been the solo designer at AristoTelos, completely redesigning a workforce management system app for web and mobile devices. Translating complex, enterprise-scale software into interfaces that feel straightforward to use.

Previously she worked at Loom on the Moon, a Prague animation studio, designing multimedia exhibition concepts for National Museum in close collaboration with curators and subject matter experts.

She holds a bachelor's degree in Information Design and a master's degree in Digital Arts.

Outside of design work, Dori lectures on AI, digital literacy, and data privacy at DOX Centre for Contemporary Arts. She also volunteers as a designer, illustrator, and lector for STROM, a non-profit educating talented children in mathematics, since the age of fifteen.
